⚡ Demand & Diversity Factors (Optional - Feature #5)
📖 NEC Article 220 - Demand factors for load calculations
Examples: Lighting=100%, Receptacles=50%, Motors=85-100% depending on duty
📖 IEEE 141-1993 Red Book - Diversity factors for system design
Auto defaults: Source Bus=90%, Distribution Bus=85%, Branch Circuit=80%
Leave blank to use automatic values
💡 What's the difference?
  • Demand Factor: Accounts for the fact that not all equipment operates at full load all the time.
  • Diversity Factor: Accounts for the fact that not all loads reach maximum demand at the same time.
  • Combined Effect: Design Load = Connected Load × Demand Factor × Diversity Factor
