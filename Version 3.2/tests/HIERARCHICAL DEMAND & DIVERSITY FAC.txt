📖 HIERARCHICAL METHODOLOGY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This calculation implements a TWO-LEVEL hierarchy:

LEVEL 1: Individual Substation Maximum Demand
  • Apply demand factors to equipment (motors, transformers, etc.)
  • Apply substation diversity factor to account for internal non-coincidence
  • Result: Maximum Demand (MD) for each substation
  • Formula: MD = (Connected × Demand Factor) / Substation DF

LEVEL 2: Total System Maximum Demand
  • Treat each substation MD as an "individual load"
  • Apply system-level diversity factor
  • Accounts for peaks at different times (offices vs. shops)
  • Result: Total system MD for sizing main utility service
  • Formula: Total MD = (Sum of Substation MDs) / System DF
