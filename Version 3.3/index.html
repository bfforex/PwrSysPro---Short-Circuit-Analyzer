⚡ Demand & Diversity Factors (Optional - Feature #5)
📖 NEC Article 220 - Demand factors for load calculations
Examples: Lighting=100%, Receptacles=50%, Motors=85-100% depending on duty
📖 IEEE 141-1993 Red Book - Diversity Factor for system design
Auto defaults: Source Bus = 1.10, Distribution Bus = 1.15, Branch Circuit = 1.20
Leave blank to use automatic values
💡 What's the difference?
  • Demand Factor (Kd): Used for an individual load or a small group. Formula:
    Maximum Demand (individual) = Connected Load × Demand Factor
    Demand Factor ≤ 1.0 (reduces connected load).
  • Diversity Factor (DF): Used when combining multiple load groups at system level. Formula:
    Maximum System Demand = (Sum of Individual Maximum Demands) ÷ Diversity Factor
    Diversity Factor ≥ 1.0 (accounts for non-simultaneous peaks).
  • Relationship: Kd = 1 ÷ DF (they are inverses, do NOT multiply both).
  • Calculation Flow:
    Step 1: Apply Demand Factor to each load → Individual Maximum Demands.
    Step 2: Sum all individual demands and apply Diversity Factor → System Maximum Demand.
